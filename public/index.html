Mint FXRP and chain an action into the same transaction. If the action fails, nothing mints — it's atomic.

Every XRPL address gets a deterministic Flare smart account. No signup, no deploy.

Mint-only, transfer, redeem, vault deposit, or bridge to another chain — pick what you need.
STEP 03
Get a quote
See the minting fee, executor fee, and exact FXRP you'll receive before you sign anything.
STEP 04
Sign one payment
Sign a single XRP Payment in your XRPL wallet. The memo encodes everything; an executor finalizes on Flare.

Built for trust

Every FXRP is backed 1:1 by XRP locked in the Core Vault. Auditable on-chain, always.

🔐
Non-custodial

Your keys, your XRP

We never see your keys. You sign in your own XRPL wallet; we only prepare the transaction and memo. Keys stay yours.

⛓️
Omnichain

FXRP on LayerZero

Bridge FXRP across Flare, Base, BNB Chain, Ethereum, and HyperEVM using LayerZero OFT — one token, many chains.

⚡
Gasless

No FLR needed

Smart-account holders can redeem FXRP to XRP without owning a single FLR. A relayer pays the Flare gas for you.

📊
Transparent

Proof of reserves

XRP locked in the Core Vault is verifiable on the XRPL ledger. The backing ratio and per-chain supply are live on the dashboard.
